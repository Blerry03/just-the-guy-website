THE PEOPLE BEHIND JUST THE GUY

Get to Know Our Family.

When you contact Just The Guy, these are some of the people helping make your project happen.

OWNER

Meet Sara.

Sara is the owner of Just The Guy Gutters & Exteriors and the person behind the family-owned business we've built here in Mid-Michigan.

She helps guide the business and the values behind it, with a focus on treating customers with respect and building something meaningful for the family.

To us, being family-owned means our name is attached to the work, and that matters.

FIELD OPERATIONS & CREW LEAD

Meet Josh.

Josh brings hands-on exterior construction experience to Just The Guy and plays a major role in the work happening out in the field.

From looking at potential projects and putting together estimates to organizing crews and helping with installations, Josh works to keep projects moving from start to finish.

His experience and practical knowledge are an important part of the work we do.

OPERATIONS & MARKETING

Meet Blue.

Blue works across several parts of Just The Guy, from communicating with customers and helping prepare estimates to marketing, scheduling, and daily operations.

Blue is also involved in fieldwork, including taking measurements, preparing gutters, coordinating work on-site, and helping make sure the details come together.

Whether it's behind a computer or out on a job site, Blue helps connect the office side of the business with the work happening at your home.
