02 / OUR SPECIALTY

Gutter Services

What gutter services do you offer?

Our gutter services include seamless gutter installation, gutter replacement, downspouts, gutter guards, gutter cleaning, and repairs.

Visit our Gutter Services page for more information.

What are seamless gutters?

Seamless gutters are custom-formed to fit the measurements of your home or building. Unlike sectional gutters, they have fewer joints along straight runs, which reduces potential leak points and provides a clean appearance.

Do you offer both 5-inch and 6-inch gutters?

Yes. We work with both 5-inch and 6-inch seamless gutter systems. The right size depends on factors such as roof area, pitch, and expected water flow.

Can I choose the color of my gutters?

Yes! Seamless gutters are available in a variety of colors. We can discuss available options to help you find something that complements your home's exterior.

Do you install gutter guards?

Yes. We offer gutter guard installation to help reduce leaves and larger debris entering your gutters.

Gutter guards can reduce maintenance, but no system eliminates the need for occasional inspection and cleaning.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

For many homes, checking and cleaning gutters at least twice a year is a good starting point, typically in the spring and fall.

Homes with heavy tree coverage may require more frequent cleaning. You can learn more in our gutter cleaning guide.

Can you repair my gutters instead of replacing them?

In many cases, yes. Depending on the condition of the system, we may be able to address issues such as leaking corners, loose sections, drainage problems, or improper pitch without replacing everything.

We can help evaluate which option makes sense for your situation.

Why are my gutters overflowing?

Overflowing gutters can be caused by clogged gutters, blocked downspouts, improper pitch, insufficient capacity, or other drainage issues.

The best solution depends on the underlying cause, so an inspection may be needed.

Can you replace just one section of gutter?

Sometimes. If the surrounding system is in good condition and a replacement section can be installed properly, a partial replacement may be possible. We'll need to evaluate the existing gutter and the area involved.

Do you install and replace downspouts?

Yes. We install and replace downspouts as part of complete gutter systems and individual drainage projects. Proper downspout placement helps direct roof runoff away from vulnerable areas around your home.

03 / WORKING WITH US

Estimates & Scheduling

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes! You can request a free estimate by contacting us through our website or calling (517) 885-8953.

Request your estimate here.

How do I request an estimate?

You can submit a request through our website, call us at (517) 885-8953, or email us.

Sharing your property location, the type of service you need, and any helpful photos can give us a better understanding of your project.

Do I need to be home for an estimate?

Not necessarily. Some exterior projects can be measured and evaluated without anyone being home, provided we have permission and safe access to the areas that need to be inspected.

If we need to discuss details with you on-site, we'll coordinate that beforehand.

How soon can you schedule my project?

Scheduling depends on our current workload, weather conditions, material availability, and the scope of your project.

We'll discuss scheduling availability with you when reviewing your project.

How long does gutter installation take?

Installation time depends on the size of the building, accessibility, roof height, number of downspouts, and whether existing gutters need to be removed.

Many residential installations can be completed in a day, while larger or more complicated projects may take longer.

What happens if the weather is bad on installation day?

Safety and installation conditions come first. Rain, high winds, ice, and other weather conditions may require us to adjust the schedule.

If weather affects your appointment, we'll work with you to coordinate another suitable time.

Do you remove and dispose of old gutters?

We can include removal and disposal of existing gutters as part of a replacement project. The exact scope will be outlined in your estimate.

How much does a new gutter system cost?

Pricing depends on the total gutter footage, building height, gutter size, downspouts, accessories, and installation conditions.

Because every property is different, we recommend requesting an estimate for accurate pricing.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment options can be discussed when your estimate is prepared. Contact us if you have a specific payment method you'd like to use.

04 / BEYOND GUTTERS

Exterior Services

Do you offer services other than gutters?

Yes. In addition to our gutter services, we can help coordinate certain exterior projects involving siding, soffit, fascia, and roofing through independent subcontractors.

Contact us with your project details to see what options are available.

Does your own crew perform siding and roofing work?

Our crew specializes in gutter installation and related services. For siding, roofing, and certain other exterior projects, we coordinate work with independent subcontractors rather than performing those services with our own gutter crew.

Can you help with damaged soffit or fascia?

We can discuss soffit and fascia projects and help coordinate appropriate exterior work when available.

The best approach depends on the extent of the damage and whether any underlying issues need to be addressed.

Can you coordinate small roofing repairs?

Depending on the project and availability, we may be able to help coordinate certain roofing repairs through independent subcontractors.

Send us a description of the problem and any photos you have so we can discuss the next steps.

Can I ask about multiple exterior services at once?

Absolutely. If you're planning gutter work along with another exterior project, let us know what you're hoping to accomplish. We can discuss what services may be available and how the work could be coordinated.
