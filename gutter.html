Seamless gutter options

5-Inch or 6-Inch Gutters?

Every roof is different. We'll help determine which gutter size makes sense based on roof area, pitch, drainage needs, and downspout placement.

5-INCH SEAMLESS GUTTERS

A Clean, Classic Fit

Five-inch seamless gutters are a common choice for residential homes. They're designed to collect roof runoff and direct it toward your downspouts.

  • Popular size for many residential applications
  • Continuous runs help reduce the number of joints along straight sections
  • Available in colors to coordinate with your home's exterior, subject to supplier availability
6-INCH SEAMLESS GUTTERS

More Room for Water

Six-inch seamless gutters offer a larger channel and can be a good fit for homes with larger roof areas or higher runoff demands.

  • Larger capacity than comparable 5-inch gutters
  • May be recommended for certain steep or complex roofs
  • Correct sizing still depends on the whole drainage system

Not sure which size you need?

That's what we're here for. We can review the roof and drainage layout and recommend an appropriate gutter and downspout combination.

Move water away

Downspouts & Extensions

Good gutters need a good way to carry water down and away from your home. We offer 2×3 and 3×4 downspout sizes, plus hinged extension options.

2 × 3 INCH

2×3 Downspouts

A standard rectangular downspout size used on many residential gutter systems. Suitable placement and quantity depend on the roof and drainage design.

3 × 4 INCH

3×4 Downspouts

A larger rectangular downspout option that can move more water than a comparable 2×3 downspout, helping support higher-flow gutter systems.

CONVENIENT ACCESS

Hinged Downspout Extensions

A hinged extension lets you lift the extension out of the way when mowing, landscaping, or accessing the area. Lower it again to direct water away during rain.

COMPLETE DRAINAGE

Downspout Placement & Connections

Elbows, outlets, and extensions work together to route runoff. We can discuss how your downspouts should discharge and whether existing drain connections need attention.

Common questions

Gutter FAQs

Are seamless gutters completely joint-free?

No. Seamless gutters minimize joints along straight runs, but corners, end caps, outlets, and other fittings still require connections.

Are 6-inch gutters always better?

Not necessarily. Six-inch gutters have more capacity, but the right size depends on the roof and the overall drainage design.

Will gutter guards mean I never have to clean my gutters?

No. Guards can reduce debris buildup, but inspections and occasional maintenance may still be needed.

Can I match my gutters to my siding or trim?

Often, yes. We can help you compare available supplier samples to your home's exterior colors.
